## 完整提示

Create one square 1024x1024 PNG with genuinely transparent background, precisely 4 columns by 4 rows of equal 256x256 invisible cells. One isolated part centered inside EACH cell with 24px minimum transparent margins. NO text, labels, grids, shadows, background, assembled character, extra items. Style: polished hand-painted Chinese wuxia indie RPG, muted jade green, warm ivory linen, dark brown ink outlines, simplified readable shapes, adult young male proportions, flat frontal orthographic view, gentle painted shading. All body limb parts vertical, joints rounded and covered for skeletal overlap. Exact row-major cells: row1: [bald male head with ears and neck, NO hair; black topknot hairstyle front piece shaped to sit on that head; alternate black tied-back hairstyle front piece; sleeveless ivory cross-collar tunic torso with brown waist belt, no head/arms/legs]. row2: [alternate sleeveless dark jade cross-collar tunic torso with brown belt; ivory upper sleeve from shoulder to elbow vertical; ivory forearm sleeve from elbow to wrist vertical; jade upper sleeve from shoulder to elbow vertical]. row3: [jade forearm sleeve from elbow to wrist vertical; closed relaxed human hand with wrist vertical; dark charcoal trouser upper leg vertical; dark charcoal trouser lower leg vertical]. row4: [dark cloth shoe side view pointing right; straight Chinese jian sword vertical with blade UP and brown grip DOWN; alternate wider steel sword vertical blade UP with jade/gold hilt DOWN; black rear ponytail hair piece vertical]. Head and torso approx 160px wide, arms approx80px wide, legs100px wide, swords thin and tall. All parts fully contained in their assigned cell. Consistent lighting and palette.
